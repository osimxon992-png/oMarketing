# Surface brief: whole site (landing first)

Scope: replacement visual world for the entire web app — landing `/` (Persuade), auth `/login` `/register`, seller dashboard and owner admin (Operate). All existing copy is kept verbatim (user decision). Dark mode is dropped (user did not keep it). 3D is live WebGL (user decision); landing and auth only, never on working screens.

Audience and job: Uzbek online sellers (small owner-run to larger shops). Action: start the 7-day free trial. Proof available: product demo content only (Nafis Kiyim sample chat, labelled as demo); no customers, numbers or testimonials exist.

Memorable moment: the clay tableau reacts to the pointer — cut-outs lift and settle — while the chat beside it turns a question into order #1042 and a payment link.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The landing is a painted bazaar tableau where clay cut-outs at deliberately wrong scales are pasted around a live chat that becomes an order and a payment. It refuses the split hero with a phone mockup above a row of equal feature cards.

OWN-WORLD: Drenched painted leaf-green field with lime brushwork; slab colours mango, guava, pomegranate-pink, lime and charcoal; every word block and control sits on its own hand-rolled clay slab, tilted a few degrees, pressed bevel plus soft cast shadow; Dela Gothic One on slabs, Onest for reading; WebGL clay objects (pomegranate, melon wedge, grape bunch, grape leaves, chat bubble, bag, payment card) in matte clay with contact shadows. Operate screens: paper-clay ground, leaf-green rail, slab cards and chips, no 3D.

STORY: A seller sees the bot answer, take the order and send the payment link in one chat, believes it can work for their shop in Uzbek or Russian, and starts the trial.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed green tableau under a cut-out nav strip. Headline as tilted slabs across the left two-thirds at mismatched sizes, "sotuvchi" largest on guava; subtitle on a paper slab; mango slab primary "7 kun bepul sinab ko'ring" plus a paper secondary. The real chat cut-out overlaps the headline's right edge; a giant 3D pomegranate behind it, grapes, melon, bag and Payme card around at wrong scales; "o / MAR / KE / TING!" syllable slabs stacked top-right.

FORM: Tropicália sleeve collage (catalog challenger posters-covers-sleeves-tropicalia-sleeve-collage, adopted by the user over grounded candidate 3, Rishton lagani). Seed 722d4bb0. Signature interaction: cut-outs lift toward the pointer and settle back; a click squashes clay. Motion grammar: lift and settle, everywhere.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
